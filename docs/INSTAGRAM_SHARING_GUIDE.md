# Instagram Product Sharing Guide

## Overview

Instagram works differently from other social media platforms. Unlike Facebook or Twitter, Instagram doesn't support direct link sharing from web browsers. This guide explains the best ways to share DukaNest products on Instagram.

## How Instagram Sharing Works

### Current Implementation

When tenants click the **Instagram** share button on a product page:
1. ✅ Product link is automatically copied to clipboard
2. ✅ Helpful tip appears explaining how to use the link
3. ✅ Link can be pasted into Instagram bio or post captions

### Why Instagram is Different

- **No Direct Link Sharing**: Instagram doesn't allow clickable links in post captions (except for verified accounts with 10K+ followers)
- **Mobile-First**: Instagram is primarily a mobile app
- **Link in Bio Strategy**: Most businesses use "Link in bio" approach
- **Instagram Shopping**: Full product tagging requires Facebook Catalog integration

## Sharing Methods

### Method 1: Link in Bio (Recommended - Works Now ✅)

**Best for:** All Instagram accounts

**Steps:**
1. Click Instagram share button on product page (link is copied automatically)
2. Go to your Instagram profile
3. Edit your bio
4. Add the product link (or use a link-in-bio service like Linktree)
5. In your post caption, write: "Link in bio 👆" or "Check bio for link"
6. Users click your bio link to view the product

**Example Post Caption:**
```
🔥 New product just dropped! 
Check out this amazing [Product Name] - $XX.XX
Link in bio 👆
#YourStore #NewProduct #ShopNow
```

**Pros:**
- ✅ Works for all accounts
- ✅ No special setup required
- ✅ Easy to update
- ✅ Can track clicks (if using link-in-bio service)

**Cons:**
- ⚠️ Extra step for customers (click bio → click link)
- ⚠️ Can only have one link at a time (unless using link-in-bio service)

### Method 2: Instagram Stories (Works Now ✅)

**Best for:** All Instagram accounts

**Steps:**
1. Create an Instagram Story with product image
2. Add text: "Swipe up" or "Link in bio"
3. For accounts with 10K+ followers: Add link sticker
4. For other accounts: Mention "Link in bio"

**Tips:**
- Use product images from your store
- Add engaging text overlays
- Use Instagram's creative tools (stickers, GIFs, polls)
- Tag your location for local discovery

### Method 3: Instagram Shopping (Advanced - Requires Setup)

**Best for:** Instagram Business accounts with Facebook Catalog

**Requirements:**
- Instagram Business account
- Facebook Business account
- Facebook Catalog with products
- Business must comply with Instagram's merchant agreement
- Account must be approved for Instagram Shopping

**Features:**
- ✅ Product tags in posts (tap to view product details)
- ✅ Shopping tab on Instagram profile
- ✅ Product stickers in Stories
- ✅ Direct checkout from Instagram (if enabled)

**Setup Process:**
1. Connect Instagram to Facebook Business Manager
2. Create Facebook Catalog (can sync from DukaNest)
3. Enable Instagram Shopping in Instagram settings
4. Wait for approval (usually 1-3 business days)
5. Start tagging products in posts

**Limitations:**
- Maximum 5 product tags per post
- Limited to 25 tagged posts per 24 hours
- Not available for Stories, Live, or IGTV
- Requires ongoing catalog maintenance

## Best Practices for Instagram

### 1. **Visual Content**
- Use high-quality product images
- Show products in use/lifestyle shots
- Use consistent aesthetic/filter
- Create product carousels (multiple images)

### 2. **Captions**
- Write engaging, authentic captions
- Include relevant hashtags (5-10 recommended)
- Use emojis strategically
- Include call-to-action: "Link in bio 👆"
- Mention price if competitive

### 3. **Hashtags**
- Use mix of popular and niche hashtags
- Include brand hashtags
- Use location hashtags for local businesses
- Research competitor hashtags

### 4. **Posting Strategy**
- Post consistently (daily or 3-5x per week)
- Best times: 11 AM - 1 PM, 7 PM - 9 PM
- Use Instagram Stories daily
- Engage with comments and DMs

### 5. **Link-in-Bio Services**
Consider using services like:
- **Linktree** - Free and paid plans
- **Carrd** - Simple link pages
- **Bio.link** - Free link aggregator
- **ManyLink** - Multiple link options

These allow you to have multiple product links in your bio.

## Quick Start Guide for Tenants

### Step 1: Set Up Your Bio
1. Click Instagram share button on a product
2. Copy the link (automatically copied)
3. Go to Instagram → Edit Profile → Bio
4. Paste link or use link-in-bio service

### Step 2: Create Your First Post
1. Take/select product photo
2. Write caption with product details
3. Add "Link in bio 👆" at the end
4. Add relevant hashtags
5. Post!

### Step 3: Use Stories
1. Create story with product image
2. Add text: "New product! Link in bio"
3. Use stickers, GIFs, polls for engagement
4. Post to story

### Step 4: Engage
1. Respond to comments
2. Answer DMs about products
3. Share user-generated content
4. Post consistently

## Troubleshooting

### Link Not Working
- Ensure link is complete (includes https://)
- Test link in browser first
- Check if link-in-bio service is working
- Verify product page is accessible

### Low Engagement
- Post at optimal times
- Use better hashtags
- Improve image quality
- Engage with followers
- Post more consistently

### Want Instagram Shopping?
- Set up Facebook Business account
- Create product catalog
- Apply for Instagram Shopping
- Consider DukaNest's future Instagram Shopping integration
