# DukaNest Landing Page Redesign

## Goal

Redesign the DukaNest homepage into a world-class SaaS landing page inspired by Shopify, Stripe, Linear, Framer, and Vercel.

The objective is to maximize signups and conversions while creating a premium, award-worthy experience.

---

# Design Principles

* Minimal and premium.
* Large typography.
* Rounded cards.
* Plenty of white space.
* Smooth animations.
* Product-first approach.
* Mobile-first responsive design.
* Blue primary color (#0B33B7).
* Soft background gradients.
* Use Inter or Sora font.
