# Dukanest Target Audience Definition

## Primary Target Audience (Core Customers)

Small business owners and entrepreneurs who sell physical products online but do not have their own website.

They currently sell through:

- WhatsApp Business
- Instagram
- Facebook Marketplace
- TikTok Shop-style selling
- Jiji
- Jumia
- Telegram groups

They are already selling products, not just thinking about starting.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Business Type

Small product-based businesses such as:

- Fashion and clothing sellers
- Cosmetics and beauty products
- Electronics accessories
- Shoes and handbags
- Home goods
- Perfumes
- Baby products
- Small retail shops
- Thrift stores
- Boutique stores

### Business Size

Typically:

- 1-3 people running the business
- Owner-operated
- Few or no employees
- Less than 200 orders/month
- Early-stage business

### Technical Level

They are:

- Comfortable using smartphones
- Use social media daily
- Can upload photos and prices
- Already manage orders digitally

They are not beginners, but they are not technical experts.

## Core Problem They Have

They sell online but their business is disorganized and inefficient.

Common problems:

- Orders lost in WhatsApp chats
- Customers asking "Price?" repeatedly
- Manual order tracking
- Hard to track stock
- No professional brand presence
- No centralized system
- Hard to scale

## Core Desire

They want:

- A professional business image
- More organized orders
- More customer trust
- Less manual work
- A real online store
- A branded business
- Affordable solution

## Biggest Belief

Most believe:

"Websites are expensive and complicated."

Dukanest wins by breaking this belief.

## Buying Motivation

They are most likely to sign up when they feel:

- Their business is growing
- Orders are becoming hard to manage
- They want to look professional
- Customers ask for a website
- They want to scale

Affordable pricing is critical.

Your pricing is positioned correctly.

## Not Our Target Audience

Very important to define.

Not targeting:

- Large enterprises
- Supermarkets
- Manufacturers
- ERP users
- Developers
- Dropshipping gurus
- Service businesses
- Restaurants
- Corporate companies

This keeps marketing focused.

## One-Sentence Target Audience Definition

Use this everywhere:

Small business owners already selling products online who want an affordable and professional ecommerce website.

## Ultra-Clear Version (Best Internal Definition)

Write this somewhere:

Dukanest is for small product sellers who already sell on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, Jiji or Jumia and want their own affordable online store.

This is your north star.

## Why This Definition Is Powerful

It guides:

- Ads
- Landing pages
- Features
- Pricing
- Messaging

Without this, SaaS startups drift.

You won't.
